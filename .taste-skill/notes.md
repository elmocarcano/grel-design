# TuttoTondo homepage — taste run notes

## Brief (my reading)
Homepage of Grel Design, a two-architect brand from Lake Como / Milano. One product so far:
TuttoTondo, a round coffee table cut from one sheet. Reader: design-minded buyer, probably
arriving from Instagram. One job: make them want it and open the configurator (or buy an edition).
Stack: single-file SPA (index.html), homepage route '/'. Real imagery: one realistic lifestyle
render (cream × bordeaux in a room above the lake), an exact-geometry renderer that can make
any product image (my own tool = "image tool" for the product), detail crop, oak render.

## Inventory (only this client has these)
1. The name: "tutto tondo" is the sculptor's term for a figure carved fully in the round,
   seen from every side.
2. One 70 × 80 cm sheet; the cut plan where the top's circle is the edge of both plates' arcs.
3. Zero waste: what one part gives up, the next takes.
4. A cross of two slotted plates; the top drops in. No screws, no tools, about a minute.
5. Ø 60 × H 40 cm.
6. The top turns and flips; the reverse can be a chess board or a card table.
7. Drawn by Greta, an architect, first for their own home; Milano; made in Italy.
8. The logo: two intersecting squares — the plates' cross seen from above.
9. Colours: eight shades (Cream, Butter, Rose, Terracotta, Sage, Lake, Bordeaux, Graphite) or any RAL.
10. Prices: €329 standard, €479 custom RAL, €499–549 oak.
11. Lake Como: the founders' lake, the view in the photograph.
12. Ships flat: the parts stack back into the sheet.

## Direction
Dealt `hero-object` (The object, large), seed 1583369997, from options uneven-photographs / split-stage / hero-object.
Why: the name is a sculptor's term for a figure seen from every side; this direction makes the table
the actor, huge and cropped, and lets it return from another angle. Split-stage was rejected because
the owner already called "text left, picture right" boring.
Palette: dealt black and crimson, proportions kept, crimson replaced by the product's own bordeaux.
Type: Bricolage Grotesque (dealt; also the brand's face), 400/500/600, sentence case.

## Idea
On this page, the sculptor's "tutto tondo" — a figure carved to be seen from every side — becomes
the object, large: the table fills the first screen cropped by three edges, is examined at three
distances, assembled verb by verb, and returns at the end from another side.
Competitor test: "another table becomes the object, large" works, but "tutto tondo becomes…" only
works for this table: its name is the brief.
Live thing: choose the plates and the top from the eight shades and see the exact table, with its price.
